Descrizione:

```markdown
## 🇮🇹 Italiano

- Drag & drop affidabile: file/cartella in qualunque punto della finestra (offerta «Apri ora»).

## 🇬🇧 English

- Reliable drag & drop anywhere on the window (optional “Open now”).
- Updater downloads to `%LocalAppData%\RTAD_Standalone\updates\` (not Downloads) with cleanup; Find copies ignores that folder.
- CD/DVD in “Whole PC”; `.cda` message. Audio CDs: rip with Windows Media Player, then analyze in RTAD.
- Wrapped reports; clearer Installer channel marker; guide updated.
- OCR/copies/Paste from 1.7.5 remain.
```
